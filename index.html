Lightstrings

Stretch threads of light between your fingertips and play the air.

  • StretchRaise both hands. Faint threads join your fingertips.
  • PullPinch thumb and index on both hands to pull a thread taut. Shorter is higher. Release to pin it.
  • PluckSwipe a fingertip across a string.
  • FlickFlick fast along a string to send sparks down it.
  • ClearMake a fist and hold it for a second.
  • DrawDrag to stretch a string. Shorter is higher.
  • PluckSwipe across strings (mouse: just move over them; touch: switch to Play).
  • FlickSwipe fast along a string to send sparks.
Hand tracking runs on your device. No video leaves it.
Best on a laptop webcam with your hands well lit.
